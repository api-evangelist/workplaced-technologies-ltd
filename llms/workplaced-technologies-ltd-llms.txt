# Workplaced

> Workplaced (Workplaced Technologies Ltd) helps companies create workplaces that work. It is a workplace-strategy and hybrid-work SaaS platform covering workplace experience surveys, office occupancy measurement, space taxonomy and planning, desk and meeting-room booking, and hybrid work policy modelling. Workplaced is a 500 Global portfolio company.

Workplaced publishes no public developer program: there is no developer portal, API documentation, or public API specification. The backend at api.workplaced.com is fully authentication-gated (all /api/* paths return 401) and serves the Workplaced application only.

## Products

- [Dynamics](https://www.workplaced.com/dynamics): Survey workplace experience, work patterns, and office activity preferences to make meaningful management decisions.
- [Occupancy](https://www.workplaced.com/occupancy): Measure office space utilisation beyond sensor data. Report on activities performed, utilisation vs capacity KPIs and more.
- [Taxonomy](https://www.workplaced.com/taxonomy): Create office space plans with workspaces that match employees' activities and needs, plus a kit of parts to build them.
- [Booking](https://www.workplaced.com/booking): Book desks, coordinate office days, reserve meeting rooms and more all from one app with the desk booking solution.
- [Balance](https://www.workplaced.com/balance): Configure your hybrid work model to evaluate how your policy can benefit employee productivity and retention.
